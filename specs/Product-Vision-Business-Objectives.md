# Product Vision & Business Objectives

Source: BRD v1.1 §1 (Executive summary), §2 (Problem, goals & KPIs).

## Vision

Businesses want a chat assistant that already knows their products, policies and FAQs, without writing a script for every question. SanchiJawab is a multi-tenant SaaS: a business owner signs up, pastes their website URL, optionally uploads files, and within minutes gets a chatbot that answers from that content with source links — customisable, embeddable in one line of code, with a shared inbox for human handoff.

**One-line promise**: paste your URL, get a branded AI assistant that answers from your own content, live on your site in under 15 minutes.

## Problem

- Visitors leave when they can't find an answer quickly; support teams answer the same questions every day.
- Generic chatbots hallucinate or answer from outside the company's own content, damaging trust.
- Enterprise tools are expensive and complex for small/mid-sized businesses, especially in price-sensitive markets like India.

## Business goals

1. Launch an MVP a non-technical owner can set up alone in under 15 minutes.
2. Resolve ≥60% of visitor questions without a human by month three post-launch.
3. Reach 100 paying workspaces within six months of public launch.
4. Keep AI cost per conversation low enough for >70% gross margin.

## KPIs (MVP targets)

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Time to first bot (sign-up → first test answer) | < 15 min (median) |
| AI resolution rate | ≥ 60% |
| Answer groundedness | ≥ 95% |
| First-token latency (p95) | < 2 s |
| Crawl success rate | ≥ 90% |
| Lead capture rate | ≥ 8% |
| Trial → paid conversion | ≥ 10% |

## Differentiation

Source citations on every answer, an "unanswered questions" report that turns gaps into new knowledge in one click, Hinglish support, and pricing aimed at small businesses (benchmarked against text.com's chat widget — see BRD §5).
