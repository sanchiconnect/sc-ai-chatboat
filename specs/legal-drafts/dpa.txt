## Parties and scope

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between the customer ("Controller" / "Data Fiduciary") and [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS] ("Processor"). It applies to personal data that visitors and customer staff put into the SanchiJawab service, which Processor handles only on the customer's behalf.

## Subject matter, nature and duration

Processor hosts the customer's chat assistant: it crawls and indexes the customer's content, answers visitor questions from that content, stores conversations and lead details, and lets the customer's team review and respond. Processing lasts for the term of the agreement plus the deletion period below.

## Types of data and people

Data: visitor chat messages, page address, anonymous visitor identifier, language, ratings, optional name, email and phone from lead forms; customer staff names, emails and roles. People: the customer's website visitors and the customer's team members. The customer must not use the service to collect special categories of data [OR: list permitted categories, per counsel].

## Processor's obligations

Process personal data only on the customer's documented instructions, which are the agreement and the customer's use of the settings and features of the service. Make sure everyone authorised to process the data is bound by confidentiality. Keep the data of each customer logically separated from every other customer's. Help the customer answer visitors' rights requests using the built-in export and erase tools, and by email within [DAYS] days where the tools do not cover the request. Help the customer with security, breach notification and impact assessments, taking into account the information available to Processor.

## Security measures

Per-customer data separation covered by automated tests; password hashing with bcrypt; encryption at rest of payment-gateway secrets; rate limiting on sign-in and public chat; protection against requests to internal network addresses; role-based access for customer teams; an audit log of platform staff actions; masking of contact details and card numbers in stored bot replies; and customer-configurable automatic deletion of old conversations.
