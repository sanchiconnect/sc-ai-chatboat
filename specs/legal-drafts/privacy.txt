## Who we are

SanchiJawab is operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS] ("we", "us"). We provide an AI chat assistant that businesses ("customers") add to their own websites. This policy covers two groups: customers who use our dashboard, and the visitors who chat with a customer's assistant. For visitor conversations the customer decides why and how the data is used (the "data fiduciary" under India's DPDP Act, or "controller" under GDPR) and we process it on their behalf (the "data processor"). Our Data Processing Addendum sets this out.

## What we collect from customers

Account details: name, work email, password (stored only as a salted bcrypt hash), workspace and business name, and team members' emails and roles. Content you give us to learn from: pages we crawl from your website and files you upload (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, text), plus the text chunks and numeric embeddings we derive from them. Billing details: plan, orders and invoice records, and GST details you enter. Card and bank details are entered with our payment providers (Razorpay or Stripe) and are never stored by us. Technical data: IP address, browser type, and the time of sign-ins and administrative actions.

## What we collect from visitors who chat

Messages typed into the chat, the page address the chat was opened on, the detected language, an anonymous visitor identifier generated in the visitor's browser, any rating given, and any contact details a visitor chooses to submit in a lead form (name, email, phone). The chat shows a notice and asks for agreement before a conversation starts, if the customer has left that setting on.

## How we use data

To crawl and index a customer's content, generate answers from it, hand conversations to a human on the customer's team, show analytics, send notifications and transcripts the customer or visitor asks for, bill customers, secure the service, and meet legal obligations. ## Security

Each customer's data is separated from every other customer's and this is covered by automated tests. Passwords are hashed, payment-gateway secrets are encrypted at rest, sign-in and public chat endpoints are rate limited, and the crawler and webhooks are blocked from reaching internal network addresses. Platform staff changes to plans, content and customer accounts are recorded in an audit log. No system is perfectly secure; we will tell affected customers and, where required, regulators, of a breach without undue delay [and within 72 hours where GDPR applies].
