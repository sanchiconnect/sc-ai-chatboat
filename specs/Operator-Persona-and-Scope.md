# Operator Persona and Scope

Source: BRD v1.1 §4 (Users & personas).

## Business owner / admin

Runs a small or mid-sized business (e-commerce store, clinic, coaching institute, SaaS). Not a developer. Wants the bot live quickly, on-brand, and wants to see what customers ask. Primary buyer.

## Website visitor

Arrives on desktop or mobile, often in a hurry, may write in Hindi, Hinglish or English. Wants a direct answer or a human, and does not want to fill long forms.

## Support agent

Works in the inbox, picks up handoffs, replies to leads, marks answers as wrong so the bot improves. Needs speed and conversation context.

## Platform super-admin (SanchiJawab team)

Monitors tenants, usage and costs, handles abuse reports, adjusts plan limits, reviews model quality dashboards. This is us — not a customer-facing role. No dedicated super-admin UI is scoped for MVP; handled via direct database/dashboard access until volume justifies one.
